I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.ust.hk/) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://junxianhe.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
